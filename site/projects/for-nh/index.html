Overview

FOR-NH is the ammonia-focused programme used by Octoply and TDL to distinguish ammonia-related development from the wider FLO-RA vision.

Immediate emphasis

Its immediate emphasis is storage, containment, thermal management, detection, controlled recovery and handling in inland and tidal waters.

  • Storage
  • Containment
  • Thermal management
  • Detection
  • Controlled recovery
  • Handling

Later extension

The programme has potential later extension to open-water storage and production-related functions.
