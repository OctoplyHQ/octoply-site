Waste, water and energy, together

We work at the level of systems architecture. We're interested in how waste, water and energy connect, rather than in optimising any one of them.

We have operated licensed floating infrastructure on the tidal Thames for close to two decades. Our ideas are tested on those working assets, where they have to meet commercial reality, social acceptance and technical scrutiny. When a project can run without us, it becomes a separate organisation. Tamesis Dock Limited was the first.

Tamesis Dock Limited

Octoply set up Tamesis Dock Limited in 2008 as a new company to run the bar on Tamesis Dock, a boat on the Albert Embankment. It later bought Battersea Barge, and now runs both venues as a separate business. Its staff are Robin's Hood's first users.

About the role

We are looking for a tradesperson with excellent DIY skills who is comfortable working on boats and can work largely unsupervised.

The Maintenance Technician should be skilled in at least one of the following:

  • heating and plumbing
  • electrical
  • joinery and metal work

Basic knowledge of at least two other trades is highly desirable.

You will need to be confident and enthusiastic enough to carry out fault-finding and basic repairs to all equipment, for example, pumps and kitchen and bar appliances. You will ideally have had continuity of employment within a single company for a minimum of 3 years if you are applying as an experienced candidate.

About the role

We are developing ship based technologies, including microbial systems that utilise waste streams as a feedstock to derive value through the expression of specialist biomacromolecules to the production of metabolic byproducts including ammonia and methane.

Your role

You will be responsible for the redesign of a former car ferry, MV Sound, and managing its conversion to a mobile fuelling, servicing and technology demonstrator platform. This work will be applied more broadly within the offshore sector.

The team you will join will be involved in setting up an onboard workshop and microbiology laboratory to facilitate work conducted by the company and partnering research organisations.

Requirements

Proficiency in all aspects of ship design and engineering, preferably with experience with cryogens, ORE and propulsor design. Capable of working on your own initiative and be able to manage/train a small but growing team of marine technicians.

Bonus

MV Sound is currently moored mid-river on the Thames and is accessed by a small tender. Therefore some boating experience on tidal waters and/or a driving license with an endorsement to tow trailers would be useful but not essential.

More information is available on request.

About the role

You will be part of a team developing a software platform that can act as a hub for other more specialised software and hardware.

Your role will be to lead the development of an agent based model to analyse individual and group dynamics, with an initial focus within and between organisations. There will be opportunities for you to establish collaborations with academics throughout the project.

About you

We are looking for someone who has worked on agent based or mathematical modelling, preferably on social or biological systems. You have an interest in data sharing and cybersecurity behaviours that may have an influence on group structures.

You are comfortable working with large data sets and be fluent in agent based or formal modelling methods, with the ability to develop new ones, with a particular focus on interactions between groups of individuals and their interactions with the environment.

More information is available on request.
